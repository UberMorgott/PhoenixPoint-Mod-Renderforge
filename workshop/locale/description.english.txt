[b]Give Phoenix Point on Windows a clearer image and smoother motion with Renderforge's upscaling, frame generation and graphics controls.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Upscaling and anti-aliasing:[/b] balance clarity and performance with DLSS Super Resolution, native-resolution DLAA, FSR or XeSS. Auto picks an available upscaler for your GPU and renderer; switch upscalers live.
[*][b]Frame generation:[/b] smoother motion with DLSS-G, FSR or XeSS-FG at 2x on DirectX 12 with an active upscaler. DLSS-G offers 3x/4x on RTX 50. Off by default.
[*][b]Sharpening:[/b] tune detail with NIS, even with the upscaler Off and the game's anti-aliasing intact. Set sharpness to 0 to disable it.
[*][b]Texture detail:[/b] automatic adjustment keeps textures detailed when upscaling from a lower resolution.
[*][b]LUT filters:[/b] choose a colour mood for tactical missions and the Geoscape, with adjustable strength. Scene only; the HUD stays untouched.
[*][b]Scene styles:[/b] give tactical missions and the Geoscape a Cartoon or PixelArt look. Adjust strength and PixelArt block size; the HUD stays untouched.
[*][b]Image sliders:[/b] tune Exposure, Black point, White point, Brightness, Contrast, Clarity, Vibrance and Saturation live under Options > Graphics. Scene only, in tactical missions and on the Geoscape; the HUD stays untouched. Tooltips show defaults.
[*][b]Easy reset:[/b] one row under Options > Graphics restores all Renderforge image and quality settings to their defaults; its tooltip lists them. Renderer, Upscaler, Quality, Frame generation and Colour vision keep your choices.
[*][b]Colour vision:[/b] Deuteranopia, Protanopia and Tritanopia correction works with LUT filters or scene styles in tactical missions and on the Geoscape. Scene only; HUD and menus stay untouched.
[*][b]Quality controls:[/b] clear darkened edges by turning off vignette in tactical missions and on the Geoscape, choose Very High shadows, sharpen angled surfaces with 16x anisotropic filtering and keep distant models detailed with LOD. Each control has a Vanilla position to restore the graphics preset's value.
[*][b]Crisp Geoscape markers:[/b] site markers stay clear as you rotate the globe, drawn after DLSS at output resolution on DirectX 11 with frame generation and colour vision off. Other combinations use the vanilla path. Markers bypass post-processing, so whites look slightly brighter.
[*][b]Renderer choice:[/b] switch between DirectX 11 and DirectX 12 in-game, then restart to apply.
[*][b]FPS controls:[/b] unlock the game's 60 FPS pin or choose a 30–300 FPS limit, including generated frames.
[*][b]Clean resolution list:[/b] Options > Screen shows each screen size once, with its highest refresh rate.
[*][b]Pixel-perfect UI:[/b] on by default for sharper text and full-resolution icons. Pixel-grid alignment and full-resolution interface textures remove the 1-pixel edge lines that other mods' icons, such as TFTV's, can show. Turn it off under Options > Screen; changes apply live. Animated panels may move in whole-pixel steps.
[*][b]Hotkeys:[/b] Ctrl+Alt+O shows the performance overlay: renderer, upscaler, mode, resolution, frame time and FPS, including real and presented FPS with frame generation. Ctrl+Alt+U toggles the upscaler off/on.
[/list]

[h1]Hardware and renderer[/h1]
[list]
[*]DLSS / DLAA: NVIDIA GeForce RTX, DirectX 11 or DirectX 12.
[*]DLSS-G: NVIDIA RTX 40 or newer, DirectX 12; 3x/4x require RTX 50.
[*]FSR / XeSS upscaling and 2x frame generation: any modern GPU, DirectX 12.
[*]NIS, LUT filters, colour vision correction, mip bias, FPS controls and overlay: any supported GPU, DirectX 11 or DirectX 12.
[*]Post-processing without an upscaler: verified on NVIDIA RTX; AMD, Intel and GTX have not yet been smoke-tested.
[*]DirectX 11 is the default. DirectX 12 is optional and experimental; changing resolution may disable DLSS.
[*]Frame generation needs windowed or borderless mode; it can add latency and artefacts. DLSS-G pauses when the game loses focus.
[/list]

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[list=1]
[*]Enable Renderforge under Mods and restart once. An update may need another restart.
[*]Options > Graphics: renderer, upscaler, quality, frame generation, sharpness, image filters and quality controls.
[*]Options > Screen: FPS limiter and Pixel-perfect UI. Mods > Renderforge: overlay and hotkey preferences only.
[*]Settings apply live except the renderer: choose it, press Apply and accept the restart. Unsupported choices are greyed out.
[/list]

[h1]Languages[/h1]
The UI follows your game language: English, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), French, German, Italian, Polish or Spanish. Tooltips explain where settings work.
