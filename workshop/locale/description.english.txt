[b]Renderforge is a graphics and renderer mod for Phoenix Point on Windows: upscaling, sharpening, frame generation, colour filters, quality controls, an FPS limiter and a performance overlay.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Upscaling and anti-aliasing:[/b] DLSS Super Resolution and native-resolution DLAA, FSR and XeSS; Auto selects an available upscaler for your GPU and renderer.
[*][b]Sharpening:[/b] NIS also works with the upscaler Off, preserving the game's anti-aliasing; set sharpness to 0 to disable it.
[*][b]Frame generation:[/b] DLSS-G, FSR and XeSS-FG at 2x on DirectX 12 with an active upscaler; DLSS-G also offers 3x/4x on RTX 50. Off by default.
[*][b]Image:[/b] LUT colour filters for tactical missions and Cartoon/PixelArt scene styles, with adjustable strength; the HUD stays unchanged.
[*][b]Colour vision:[/b] Deuteranopia, Protanopia and Tritanopia correction, combined with LUT filters or scene styles. Tactical scene only; HUD and menus stay unchanged.
[*][b]Quality controls:[/b] disable tactical vignette, select Very High shadows, force 16x anisotropic filtering and increase distant model detail with LOD. Each Vanilla position restores the graphics preset's value.
[*][b]Renderer:[/b] switch between DirectX 11 and DirectX 12 in-game; requires a restart.
[*][b]FPS and fonts:[/b] removes the vanilla 60 FPS pin; optional 30–300 FPS limit includes generated frames. Crisp fonts for supported dynamic interface text.
[*][b]Hotkeys:[/b] Ctrl+Alt+O toggles the benchmark overlay (renderer, upscaler, resolution, frame time and FPS); Ctrl+Alt+U toggles the upscaler off/on.
[/list]

[h1]Hardware and renderer[/h1]
[list]
[*]DLSS / DLAA: NVIDIA GeForce RTX, DirectX 11 or DirectX 12.
[*]DLSS-G: NVIDIA RTX 40 or newer, DirectX 12; 3x/4x require RTX 50.
[*]FSR / XeSS upscaling and 2x frame generation: any modern GPU, DirectX 12.
[*]NIS, LUT filters, colour vision correction, mip bias, FPS controls and overlay: any supported GPU, DirectX 11 or DirectX 12.
[*]Post-processing without an upscaler: verified on NVIDIA RTX; AMD, Intel and GTX have not yet been smoke-tested.
[*]DirectX 11 is the default. DirectX 12 is optional and experimental; changing resolution may disable DLSS.
[*]Frame generation needs windowed or borderless mode; it can add latency and artefacts. DLSS-G pauses when the game loses focus.
[/list]

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[list=1]
[*]Enable Renderforge under Mods and restart once. An update may require another restart.
[*]Options > Graphics: renderer, upscaler, quality, frame generation, sharpness, image filters and quality controls.
[*]Options > Screen: FPS limiter and crisp fonts. Mods > Renderforge: overlay and hotkey preferences only.
[*]Settings apply live except the renderer: choose it, press Apply and accept the restart. Unsupported choices are greyed out.
[/list]

[h1]Languages[/h1]
The mod's UI follows the game language: English, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), French, German, Italian, Polish and Spanish. Tooltips state where each setting works.
